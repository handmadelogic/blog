## Anti-Patterns Verdict

No AI slop. The handwrite clip-path system, tilted cards, Studio Notebook palette, and authored voice are all distinctive. The same watercolor blob SVG path is reused four times with color-only changes — barely perceptible but the one pattern tell.

Manual detection (detect.mjs unavailable):
- Side-stripe borders: PASS (blockquote fixed to amber bg)
- Gradient text: PASS
- Em dashes: FAIL — 2 instances in About.tsx h2 lines 93, 95
- data-handwrite consistency: FAIL — crafts page annotation missing
- data-btn attribute: Minor inconsistency (="true" vs bare)

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Nine href="#" links go nowhere** — 5 post cards in LatestPosts + 4 social links in Footer. On click, page scrolls to top with no feedback. Fix: replace dead post links with /posts or add coming-soon affordance; remove anchor wrapper from social links until real URLs exist.

**[P1] All five freebie downloads 404** — /downloads/*.pdf files don't exist. "Get it" button fails for all available items. Fix: upload files or mark all as comingSoon: true.

**[P1] Em dashes in homepage About h2** — About.tsx lines 93, 95: "I make things — mostly badly — until I make them — well." Fix: "I make things, mostly badly, until I make them well."

**[P2] No skip-to-main-content link** — 7 tab stops before content on every page. Fails WCAG 2.4.1 (Bypass Blocks, Level A). Fix: visually-hidden skip link as first focusable element.

**[P2] Cartoon placeholder visible in About story section** — third column at lg+ shows dashed-border div + "cartoon goes here ✿ / swap with your SVG" to all visitors. Fix: remove third column from grid until illustration is ready.
